# Paper B — Independent Research Workspace

## Official title

**Equivariant Bifurcation, Isotropy Classification, Representation-Resolved Stability, and Nonlinear Branch Structure in Self-Consistent Quantum Interaction Networks**

## Research identity

Paper B is being redesigned as an independent nonlinear equivariant-bifurcation paper. It is **not** a continuation of the former linear-response-obstruction manuscript with a new title.

The former manuscript is preserved verbatim at:
- `source_snapshots/paper-b/paper_02_manuscript_original.md`

That snapshot is archival evidence only. Its conclusions, calculations, and numerical observations are not automatically adopted as results of the redesigned Paper B.

## New scope

The research target is the existence, symmetry/isotropy classification, representation-resolved stability, and local nonlinear structure of nonuniform fixed-point branches in the actual finite-dimensional quantum mutual-information feedback model.

See:
- `working/paper-b/PAPER_B_SCOPE_CHARTER.md` — authoritative scope and exclusions

## Status policy

This workspace is a research specification, not a claim that the title's theorem families have already been proved. Every result must be labelled **PROVEN**, **FORMALLY VERIFIED**, **PARTIALLY PROVEN**, **OPEN**, **CONJECTURAL**, or **NOT YET INVESTIGATED**. Standard equivariant bifurcation theorems may be invoked only after their regularity, representation, transversality, and nondegeneracy hypotheses have been verified for the quantum feedback map.
