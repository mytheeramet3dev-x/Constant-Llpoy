## Theorem

Let all six edge couplings be strictly positive, and let `N_-` denote the numerator of the Stage V-B expression for `P_-`. Then every point of the zero set `N_-=0` in the positive-coupling cone is a regular point: the gradient of `N_-` with respect to the six original couplings is nonzero.

Consequently, the zero set is a smooth embedded hypersurface (locally a codimension-one smooth submanifold) throughout the positive-coupling cone. In particular, there are no critical/singular points of this defining function on the zero set.
