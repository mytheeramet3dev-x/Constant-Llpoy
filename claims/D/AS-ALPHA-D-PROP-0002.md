## Proposition

Let
\[
H(h)=-\sum_{1\le i<j\le4}J_{ij}Z_iZ_j-h\sum_{i=1}^4X_i,
\qquad J_{ij}>0,
\]
and consider the analytic ground-state branch in the global bit-flip-even sector near \(h=0\). Let \(\rho_{12}(h)\) be its two-spin reduced density matrix. The spectra of the two blocks of \(\rho_{12}(h)\) obtained by resolving the eigenvalue of \(X_1X_2\) are even functions of \(h\).
