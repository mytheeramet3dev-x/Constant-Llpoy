```markdown
### Definition 1 — Interior critical point

For a fixed \(h>0\), an interior critical point \((x_c,g_c)\), with \(x_c>0\), satisfies

\[
F(x_c,g_c,h)=0,
\qquad
\partial_xF(x_c,g_c,h)=0.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
f(x_c;\eta_c)=0,
\qquad
\partial_xf(x_c;\eta_c)=0,
\qquad
\eta_c=\frac{g_c}{h}.
\]
```
